        = Prices and calculations

        Regular and promo prices are reported source values. Savings are calculated from that pair. Unit-price estimates need a known unit basis for a fair comparison; a missing basis is always marked.

        ↶ Compare like with like

        A useful price comparison needs the same store, product and package. When past prices or units are missing, we’ll say so. Your searches don’t save price history.

        Before you search

        Privacy and use notes.

        Privacy

        This page keeps your ZIP code, chosen store, search words, results and shopping list in memory for this visit. This site sends the details needed for current-price searches to Kroger, or looks up past prices in its separate history service. Browsing needs no customer account. Optional cart access requires your separate Kroger sign-in and an explicit add action. No payment or checkout is handled here. Read Kroger’s privacy notice for its separate handling.

        Product images load directly from Kroger. Your browser sends the image address, IP address and browser information to that host; browser settings may also allow existing Kroger cookies. This page sends no referring-page address for images. Product and cart links open Kroger under its own privacy notice.

        Browsing does not save your searches, results or list, build price history, or send them to advertising, email or AI services. Any past prices shown come from a separate, permitted dataset.

        If the operator enables Cart, this installation uses a secure visit cookie and keeps the connection token in server memory. Connection access expires within 30 minutes; expired sessions are removed during periodic cleanup or restart. Closing this page clears its list, but the server connection can remain until expiry. Disconnect removes this installation’s session; it does not undo cart changes or revoke permissions at Kroger.

        If you use an assistant, its provider has separate privacy terms. Using an assistant does not grant permission to send Kroger data to that provider.

        Hosting and logs

        At aipersonalized.io, aipersonalized operates this installation, with Cloudflare handling traffic before it reaches the server. Cloudflare, hosting services and error records can include your IP address, requested page, browser details, referrer, time and response status to run and troubleshoot the service.

        For this app at aipersonalized.io, successful browsing requests aren’t logged by the site’s proxy. Failure diagnostics keep response status and timing, without search values, page addresses or sign-in codes. These service logs have a size limit rather than a guaranteed deletion date; that limit does not control Cloudflare’s retention. Hosting records are separate from this page’s temporary list.

        If you use another address, ask that installation’s operator about its hosting and logs.

        Use notes

        Use this app for your own store and product searches. Follow applicable law and Kroger’s API Terms and Acceptable Use rules.

        Do not bulk-collect, archive, alter or resell API content, track customer searches or locations, or compare Kroger prices with other retailers.

        This is a free browsing tool. Confirm prices, stock and offer conditions with the store before buying.
